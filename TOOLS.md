# Tools

## Public (16) — no account needed

| Tool | Title | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `find_lounges_by_access` | Find lounges by access | Find all airport lounges accessible with a specific credit card, membership program, airline club, alliance status, or ticket class. |
| `find_lounges_by_amenity` | Find lounges by amenity | Find airport lounges that have an amenity: showers, quiet zones, family areas, fast WiFi, premium dining or spa services. |
| `find_nearby_lounges` | Lounges near a location | Find airport lounges near a latitude and longitude, nearest first, with the distance in miles. |
| `get_airport_lounges` | Lounges at an airport | Get all airport lounges at a specific airport by IATA code. |
| `get_brand_lounges` | Lounges of an airline brand | List every lounge of one airline lounge brand, grouped by airport. |
| `get_lounge_busyness` | How busy a lounge is | Get how busy a lounge usually is: the typical crowd level, the busiest and quietest hours today in the lounge's local time, the hourly pattern for each day of the week, and a live reading when one is available. |
| `get_lounge_details` | Lounge details | Get detailed information about a specific airport lounge by its slug. |
| `get_network_lounges` | Lounges in a network | Get all lounges in a specific lounge network. |
| `get_public_passport` | Someone's public lounge passport | Get another member's public lounge passport by username: their stats, country stamps, visit history and published reviews. |
| `list_access_methods` | List access methods | List all available lounge access methods including credit cards (Amex Platinum, Chase Sapphire Reserve, etc.), membership programs (Priority Pass, LoungeKey, etc.), airline clubs, alliance status levels, and ticket classes. |
| `list_lounge_brands` | List airline lounge brands | List the airline lounge brands in the directory (Emirates, Qantas, Lufthansa, Turkish Airlines, Cathay Pacific, Qatar Airways, Singapore Airlines SilverKris and more), with the number of lounges and airports for each. |
| `list_networks` | List lounge networks | List all lounge networks with their descriptions and total lounge counts. |
| `lounge_browser` | Lounge Finder | Open the interactive Lounge Finder. |
| `search_airports` | Search airports | Find airports that have lounges, with the number of lounges at each. |
| `search_lounges` | Search lounges | Search airport lounges worldwide by name, airport name, IATA code, city, or country. |
| `submit_feedback` | Send feedback to Airport Lounge List | Send a bug report, feature request or general feedback to the Airport Lounge List team. |

## Your account (14) — OAuth required

| Tool | Title | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `add_to_wishlist` | Add a lounge to my wishlist | Add a lounge to your wishlist. |
| `delete_review` | Delete my lounge review | Delete your review of a lounge. |
| `delete_visit` | Delete a logged visit | Delete one of your logged lounge visits. |
| `get_my_passport` | My lounge passport | Get your lounge passport — stats (lounges visited, airports, countries, reviews), country stamps, full visit history, and your reviews. |
| `get_my_profile` | My profile | Get your Airport Lounge List profile — username, stats (reviews, visits, wishlisted lounges), and recent activity. |
| `get_my_reviews` | My reviews | Get your lounge reviews, including pending ones. |
| `get_my_visits` | My lounge visits | Get your visited lounges list with dates and notes. |
| `get_my_wishlist` | My lounge wishlist | Get your lounge wishlist — lounges you want to visit. |
| `mark_lounge_visited` | Log a lounge visit | Log a lounge visit (check in). |
| `remove_from_wishlist` | Remove a lounge from my wishlist | Remove a lounge from your wishlist. |
| `update_passport_settings` | Change my passport settings | Change the user's lounge passport settings: their username and whether their passport is public at airportloungelist.com/passport/<username>. |
| `update_review` | Update my lounge review | Update your review of a lounge — change the rating and/or the text. |
| `update_visit` | Update a logged visit | Update one of your logged lounge visits — change the rating, notes, or date. |
| `write_review` | Write a lounge review | Write a review for a lounge. |
