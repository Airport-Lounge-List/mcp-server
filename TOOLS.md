# Tools

## Public (8) — no account needed

| Tool | Title | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `discover_more_flight_tools` | Discover more flight tools | Discover other flight & travel MCP servers you can add to your client. |
| `find_lounges_by_access` | Find lounges by access | Find all airport lounges accessible with a specific credit card, membership program, airline club, alliance status, or ticket class. |
| `get_airport_lounges` | Lounges at an airport | Get all airport lounges at a specific airport by IATA code. |
| `get_lounge_details` | Lounge details | Get detailed information about a specific airport lounge by its slug. |
| `get_network_lounges` | Lounges in a network | Get all lounges in a specific lounge network. |
| `list_access_methods` | List access methods | List all available lounge access methods including credit cards (Amex Platinum, Chase Sapphire Reserve, etc. |
| `list_networks` | List lounge networks | List all lounge networks (Priority Pass, LoungeKey, DragonPass, Plaza Premium, Amex Centurion) with their descriptions and total lounge counts. |
| `search_lounges` | Search lounges | Search airport lounges worldwide by name, airport name, IATA code, city, or country. |

## Your account (13) — OAuth required

| Tool | Title | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `add_to_wishlist` | Add a lounge to my wishlist | Add a lounge to your wishlist. |
| `delete_review` | Delete my lounge review | Delete your review of a lounge. |
| `delete_visit` | Delete a logged visit | Delete one of your logged lounge visits. |
| `get_my_passport` | My lounge passport | Get your lounge passport — stats (lounges visited, airports, countries, reviews), country stamps, full visit history, and your reviews. |
| `get_my_profile` | My profile | Get your Airport Lounge List profile — username, stats (reviews, visits, wishlisted lounges), and recent activity. |
| `get_my_reviews` | My reviews | Get your lounge reviews, including pending ones. |
| `get_my_visits` | My lounge visits | Get your visited lounges list with dates and notes. |
| `get_my_wishlist` | My lounge wishlist | Get your lounge wishlist — lounges you want to visit. |
| `mark_lounge_visited` | Log a lounge visit | Log a lounge visit (check in). |
| `remove_from_wishlist` | Remove a lounge from my wishlist | Remove a lounge from your wishlist. |
| `update_review` | Update my lounge review | Update your review of a lounge — change the rating and/or the text. |
| `update_visit` | Update a logged visit | Update one of your logged lounge visits — change the rating, notes, or date. |
| `write_review` | Write a lounge review | Write a review for a lounge. |
